## Title

Lead Capture Automation with n8n, Google Sheets & Gmail

## Project type

Personal portfolio demo

## Description

Built a simple inquiry-handling workflow for a small-business demo. When a visitor submits a contact form, n8n saves the inquiry to Google Sheets, sends a personalized confirmation email, and notifies the business owner with the lead details.

The workflow uses four standard nodes, Google OAuth connections, required form fields, and dynamic data mapping. A real manual test confirmed that the spreadsheet row and both emails contained the expected details. The project includes a reusable workflow template and setup documentation.

## Skills demonstrated

n8n workflow automation, Google Sheets integration, Gmail integration, OAuth configuration, form handling, data mapping, and end-to-end manual testing.
